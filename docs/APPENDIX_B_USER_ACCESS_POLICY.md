# Appendix B: User Access & Data Use Policy

**CareAccess SaaS Platform**

---

## B.1 Purpose

This policy defines how users of subscribing nonprofit organizations may access and use CareAccess. It ensures that access aligns with organizational roles, protects child and family confidentiality, and supports ethical and compliant use of data in a multi-tenant SaaS environment.

---

## B.2 Authorized Users

Access to CareAccess is limited to:
- Employees of subscribing organizations
- Authorized contractors or volunteers
- Organizational leadership and administrators

Each user must:
- Use unique, individual credentials
- Complete required confidentiality and privacy training
- Acknowledge this policy before access is granted

---

## B.3 Organizational Scope

- Users may access only data belonging to their organization
- Access across organizations is technically blocked
- Organization administrators manage user access within their tenant

---

## B.4 Role-Based Access Levels

### 1. Field Staff / Caseworkers

- View assigned children, families, and cases
- View activity summaries and compliance indicators
- No bulk exports
- No administrative privileges

---

### 2. Supervisors / Program Leadership

- View program-level data
- Run standard reports
- View dashboards and trends
- Limited export permissions as approved by the organization

---

### 3. Organization Administrators

- Manage users and roles within their organization
- Review audit logs
- Approve report exports
- Configure organization settings

---

### 4. Platform Administrators (Melken TechWork)

- Maintain infrastructure and security
- No access to customer case data except as contractually permitted for support
- Access is logged, restricted, and governed by internal policy

---

## B.5 Appropriate Use

Users may access CareAccess data only for:
- Supporting children and families
- Managing and overseeing programs
- Compliance and reporting obligations

The following are **prohibited**:
- Accessing records without a legitimate work purpose
- Sharing credentials or exported data
- Downloading data to unsecured or personal devices
- Attempting to access data from other organizations

---

## B.6 Reporting & Exports

- Export permissions are role-based and organization-controlled
- All exports are logged
- Exported data must be stored and shared in accordance with organizational policy
- External sharing requires management approval

---

## B.7 Monitoring & Enforcement

- All user activity is monitored for compliance
- Violations may result in access suspension or termination
- Serious violations may trigger investigation and customer notification

---

## B.8 Acknowledgement

By accessing CareAccess, users acknowledge their responsibility to protect the privacy, dignity, and confidentiality of children and families and to use the platform in alignment with their organization's mission and policies.
