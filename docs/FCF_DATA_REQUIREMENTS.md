# Friends of Children & Families - Comprehensive Data Requirements

## Executive Summary

FCF's mission is to provide safe, nurturing environments for at-risk children and families. This document outlines the comprehensive data model needed to track every aspect of a child's journey through FCF's programs, from intake to successful outcomes.

## Core Philosophy

> "If children are provided with love, a sense of security, structure, and positive opportunities for growing in a family and community atmosphere, then healthy development will occur."

Our data model must support this philosophy by capturing:
- **The whole child**: Physical, emotional, behavioral, educational, and social aspects
- **Family context**: Home environment, relationships, support systems
- **Progress tracking**: Goals, interventions, outcomes
- **Safety & wellbeing**: Incidents, risks, protective factors

---

## 1. CHILD PROFILE - The Heart of the System

### Basic Information
- **Demographics**: Name, DOB, age, gender, race/ethnicity, preferred language
- **Photo**: Current photo for identification and connection
- **Identifiers**: SSN (encrypted), Medicaid ID, school ID

### Why They're Here (Referral & Background)
- **Reason for referral**: Abuse, neglect, behavioral issues, family crisis, etc.
- **Presenting issues**: Specific problems at intake
- **Referral source**: DCF, school, family, court, hospital, etc.
- **Custody status**: Foster care, kinship care, biological family, group home
- **Legal status**: Court orders, dependency status, parental rights

### Medical & Health
- **Primary care physician**: Name, contact, last visit
- **Medications**: Current prescriptions, dosage, schedule, side effects
- **Allergies**: Food, medication, environmental
- **Medical conditions**: Chronic illnesses, disabilities, special needs
- **Immunization records**: Up-to-date status
- **Dental/Vision**: Last checkups, ongoing needs
- **Mental health diagnoses**: ADHD, depression, anxiety, PTSD, etc.
- **Therapy/Counseling**: Current providers, frequency, progress

### Behavioral & Emotional Profile
- **Behavioral concerns**: Aggression, self-harm, running away, substance use
- **Emotional state**: Depression, anxiety, mood swings, trauma responses
- **Triggers**: What sets them off (loud noises, certain topics, situations)
- **Coping mechanisms**: Healthy and unhealthy
- **Trauma history**: Abuse, neglect, loss, witnessing violence
- **Attachment style**: Secure, anxious, avoidant, disorganized

### Education & School
- **Current school**: Name, address, contact
- **Grade level**: Current grade, age-appropriate?
- **Academic performance**: GPA, reading level, math level, struggling subjects
- **Attendance**: Days present, absences, tardies, truancy issues
- **Behavior at school**: Suspensions, detentions, positive reports
- **IEP/504 Plan**: Special education services, accommodations
- **Teacher feedback**: Regular reports from teachers
- **Extracurricular activities**: Sports, clubs, interests
- **Homework completion**: Struggles, support needed

### Social & Relationships
- **Peer relationships**: Friends, social skills, bullying (victim or perpetrator)
- **Family relationships**: Siblings, parents, extended family, quality of relationships
- **Mentors**: Positive adult relationships
- **Social skills**: Communication, conflict resolution, empathy
- **Community involvement**: Church, sports teams, community groups

### Interests, Likes & Strengths
- **Hobbies**: What they love to do
- **Talents**: Art, music, sports, academics
- **Favorite activities**: Games, movies, books, food
- **Strengths**: Resilience, kindness, humor, creativity
- **Dreams/Goals**: What they want to be when they grow up
- **Motivators**: What gets them excited and engaged

### Dislikes, Fears & Challenges
- **Dislikes**: Foods, activities, situations they avoid
- **Fears**: Specific phobias, anxieties, worries
- **Challenges**: Areas where they struggle
- **Sensory sensitivities**: Loud noises, textures, lights
- **Avoidance behaviors**: What they run from

## 2. FAMILY PROFILE

### Family Structure
- **Family composition**: Parents, siblings, extended family living in home
- **Family type**: Biological, foster, kinship, adoptive, group home
- **Primary caregiver**: Who is responsible for the child
- **Custody arrangements**: Legal custody, visitation schedules

### Home Environment
- **Living situation**: House, apartment, shelter, unstable housing
- **Home safety**: Safe, clean, adequate space, hazards
- **Neighborhood**: Safe, resources available, community support
- **Household income**: Financial stability, employment status
- **Public assistance**: SNAP, TANF, housing assistance

### Family Dynamics
- **Parenting style**: Authoritative, permissive, neglectful, abusive
- **Family relationships**: Healthy, conflicted, abusive, supportive
- **Communication patterns**: Open, closed, hostile
- **Family strengths**: Resilience, love, support
- **Family stressors**: Poverty, substance abuse, mental illness, domestic violence

### Support Network
- **Extended family**: Grandparents, aunts, uncles, cousins
- **Community support**: Church, neighbors, friends
- **Professional support**: Therapists, case managers, mentors
- **Resources accessed**: Food banks, counseling, job training

---

## 3. CASE MANAGEMENT

### Case Information
- **Case number**: Unique identifier
- **Case status**: Open, active, closed, transferred
- **Intake date**: When case opened
- **Assigned worker**: Primary case manager
- **Program**: Counseling, Group Home, Mentoring, Family Support
- **Case priority**: High, medium, low risk

### Case Notes & Documentation
- **Progress notes**: Daily/weekly observations
- **Contact logs**: Phone calls, visits, emails with family/child
- **Incident reports**: Behavioral incidents, safety concerns
- **Court reports**: Updates for dependency hearings
- **Supervisor notes**: Case reviews, recommendations

### Services Provided
- **Counseling**: Individual, family, group therapy
- **Case management**: Coordination of services
- **Life skills training**: Cooking, budgeting, job skills
- **Educational support**: Tutoring, homework help
- **Recreational activities**: Summer camp, field trips
- **Transportation**: To appointments, school, activities
- **Emergency assistance**: Food, clothing, shelter

---

## 4. ASSESSMENTS & EVALUATIONS

### Initial Assessment (Intake)
- **Comprehensive evaluation**: Physical, emotional, behavioral, educational
- **Risk assessment**: Safety concerns, abuse/neglect indicators
- **Strengths assessment**: Child and family assets
- **Needs assessment**: What services are needed

### Ongoing Assessments
- **Behavioral assessments**: CBCL, Conners, trauma screening
- **Educational assessments**: Academic testing, IQ testing
- **Mental health evaluations**: Psychiatric evaluations, therapy progress
- **Family assessments**: Home visits, family functioning

### Outcome Measures
- **Goal achievement**: Progress toward case plan goals
- **Behavioral improvements**: Reduction in incidents
- **Academic progress**: Grade improvements, attendance
- **Family stability**: Reunification success, placement stability
- **Life skills**: Independence, self-sufficiency

---

## 5. GOALS & TREATMENT PLANNING

### Case Plan Goals
- **Safety goals**: Ensure child safety, prevent re-abuse
- **Behavioral goals**: Reduce aggression, improve coping
- **Educational goals**: Improve grades, increase attendance
- **Family goals**: Reunification, strengthen relationships
- **Life skills goals**: Independence, job readiness

### Goal Tracking
- **Target dates**: When goal should be achieved
- **Progress updates**: Regular check-ins on progress
- **Barriers**: What's preventing goal achievement
- **Interventions**: What's being done to help
- **Success criteria**: How we know goal is met

---

## 6. BEHAVIORAL INCIDENTS & SAFETY

### Incident Tracking
- **Date/Time**: When incident occurred
- **Type**: Aggression, self-harm, property destruction, elopement
- **Severity**: Minor, moderate, severe, critical
- **Description**: What happened
- **Trigger**: What led to the incident
- **Intervention**: How staff responded
- **Outcome**: How it was resolved
- **Follow-up**: What happens next

### Safety Planning
- **Risk factors**: What increases risk
- **Protective factors**: What keeps child safe
- **Crisis plan**: What to do in emergency
- **Emergency contacts**: Who to call
- **Medications**: PRN medications for crisis

---

## 7. OUTCOMES & SUCCESS METRICS

### Child Outcomes
- **Behavioral improvements**: Fewer incidents, better coping
- **Emotional wellbeing**: Reduced depression/anxiety
- **Academic success**: Better grades, graduation
- **Social skills**: Better relationships, fewer conflicts
- **Life skills**: Independence, job readiness

### Family Outcomes
- **Reunification**: Successful return home
- **Placement stability**: Fewer moves, permanency
- **Family functioning**: Better communication, less conflict
- **Parent skills**: Improved parenting, less stress

### Program Outcomes
- **Service completion**: Finished program successfully
- **Goal achievement**: Met case plan goals
- **Satisfaction**: Child and family satisfaction with services
- **Long-term success**: Thriving 6 months, 1 year, 5 years post-discharge

---

## 8. REPORTING & ANALYTICS NEEDS

### Dashboard Metrics (What We Built)
- **Active cases**: Current caseload
- **Intakes**: New cases this month
- **Closures**: Cases closed this month
- **Overdue**: Cases past review date

### Additional Reports Needed
- **Child demographics**: Age, gender, race breakdown
- **Referral sources**: Where kids are coming from
- **Presenting issues**: Why kids need services
- **Service utilization**: What services are being used
- **Outcomes by program**: Success rates by program type
- **Worker caseloads**: How many cases per worker
- **Length of stay**: How long kids are in program
- **Placement stability**: Number of moves
- **School performance**: Academic progress
- **Behavioral trends**: Incident patterns
- **Family engagement**: Participation in services
- **Cost per child**: Program efficiency
