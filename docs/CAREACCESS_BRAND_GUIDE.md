
# CareAccess Brand Style Guide
Owned & Operated by Melken TechWork

## Brand Essence
CareAccess is a nonprofit-focused SaaS platform designed to support organizations that serve children and families.
The brand should communicate **warmth, protection, trust, and clarity**.

## Logo Concept
The CareAccess logo icon forms a **heart-shaped pathway**, symbolizing:
- Care and compassion for children and families
- Safe, intentional access to sensitive information
- Trust and ethical stewardship

## Logo Usage
- Primary logo icon should appear on login screens, dashboards, and mobile app icons
- Do not distort, rotate, or recolor the logo
- Maintain adequate white space around the logo

## Color Palette
Primary:
- Teal: #1F6F78 (Primary brand color)
- Navy: #243A5E (Text and headings)

Secondary:
- Sage: #9DB8A0
- Warm Gray: #6B7280
- Sand: #F4F1EC

## Typography
Primary Font:
- Sans-serif (Inter, Source Sans, or system default)
- Rounded, highly readable

Usage:
- Headings: Bold
- Body text: Regular
- Avoid decorative or sharp-edged fonts

## Tone & Voice
- Calm and reassuring
- Mission-driven
- Non-technical language
- Child- and family-respectful

## Accessibility
- Maintain sufficient color contrast
- Avoid color-only indicators for status
- Use clear labels and supportive language

## File Assets
- SVG: Scalable vector for web and print
- PNG: Raster icon for app and presentations
