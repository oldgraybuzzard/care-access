# CareAccess Trust & Security

**CareAccess** is a secure, nonprofit-focused SaaS platform developed and operated by **Melken TechWork**. It is designed to support organizations that serve children and families by providing safe, role-based access to case information, reporting, and insights.

We recognize that trust is essential when working with sensitive information about children and families. CareAccess is built with privacy, ethics, and accountability as foundational principles.

---

## Our Commitments

- ✅ **Customer data always belongs to the customer**
- ✅ **Strict isolation between subscribing organizations**
- ✅ **Role-based access with audit logging**
- ✅ **No data monetization, resale, or AI training**
- ✅ **Designed for nonprofit governance and grant compliance**

---

## Security Practices

### Encryption
- **In transit:** TLS encryption for all data transmission
- **At rest:** Encryption for stored data

### Authentication & Access
- **Secure authentication** and session management
- **Role-based access control (RBAC)** - users see only what they need
- **Continuous monitoring and logging** of all access

### Infrastructure
- Modern, secure cloud platforms
- Regular security updates and patches
- Monitored services with uptime targets

---

## Ethical Stewardship

CareAccess follows a **child-first data ethic**, ensuring information is accessed only to support:
- ✅ Care delivery
- ✅ Compliance and reporting
- ✅ Accountability and oversight

**Never** for:
- ❌ Curiosity or convenience
- ❌ Commercial purposes
- ❌ Data monetization

---

## Data Ownership & Control

### Customer Ownership
All data entered into or accessed through CareAccess remains the **exclusive property of the subscribing organization**.

### Tenant Isolation
CareAccess is a multi-tenant SaaS platform with **strict organizational data separation**. Cross-tenant access is technically and contractually prohibited.

### Customer Control
- Organizations control user access within their system
- All access is logged and auditable
- Customers may review audit activity for governance or compliance

---

## Compliance & Readiness

### SOC 2 Alignment
CareAccess aligns with SOC 2 Trust Service Criteria:
- **Security:** RBAC, JWT auth, audit logs, encryption
- **Availability:** 99.9% uptime target, monitored services
- **Confidentiality:** Tenant isolation, least-privilege access
- **Processing Integrity:** Read-only by default, source-of-record clarity
- **Privacy:** Purpose-limited use, retention awareness

### HIPAA-Adjacent Safeguards
While CareAccess is not marketed as a HIPAA system, it aligns with many safeguards:
- Access controls
- Audit controls
- Transmission security
- Minimum necessary data access

This positioning is ideal for nonprofits that handle sensitive child and family information without over-claiming compliance.

## Transparency & Accountability

### Audit Logging
All user activity is logged, including:
- Login/logout events
- Data access
- Report generation
- Export activities
- Administrative changes

### Incident Response
- Security events are logged and investigated
- Customers are notified promptly of confirmed incidents
- Post-incident reviews are conducted and documented

---

## Data Processing

### Purpose-Limited Use
Melken TechWork processes data solely to:
- Provide secure access to information
- Generate reports and dashboards
- Support customer operations and compliance

### No Monetization
Customer data is **never**:
- Sold or shared with third parties
- Used for advertising
- Used to train AI models
- Monetized in any way

### Data Minimization
- Only necessary data is cached for performance and reporting
- Source systems remain authoritative
- Read-only by default to protect data integrity

---

## Support & Access

### Platform Administration
- Melken TechWork staff do not access customer data by default
- Support access requires customer authorization
- All platform-level access is logged and reviewed

### Customer Support
- Support is provided through secure channels
- Access is granted only with customer permission
- All support activity is documented
