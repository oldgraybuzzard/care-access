# Data Processing Addendum (DPA)

**CareAccess Platform**  
Owned & Operated by Melken TechWork

---

## Purpose

This Data Processing Addendum ("DPA") forms part of the CareAccess Terms of Service and governs the processing of personal data by Melken TechWork on behalf of subscribing nonprofit organizations ("Customers").

---

## Definitions

### Roles

**Customer (Data Controller)**
- The subscribing nonprofit organization
- Determines the purposes and means of processing personal data
- Responsible for compliance with applicable data protection laws

**Melken TechWork (Data Processor)**
- Processes personal data on behalf of the Customer
- Follows Customer instructions and contractual obligations
- Implements appropriate technical and organizational measures

### Data Types

**Personal Data**
- Any information relating to an identified or identifiable individual
- Includes child and family information accessed through CareAccess

**Sensitive Data**
- Information about children and families
- Case management data
- Service delivery records
- Health and social services information

---

## Processing Purpose & Scope

### Purpose

Melken TechWork processes personal data **solely** to:
- ✅ Provide secure access to information
- ✅ Generate reports and dashboards
- ✅ Support customer operations and compliance
- ✅ Maintain and improve the CareAccess platform

### Scope

**Data Subjects:** Children, families, and individuals served by Customer organizations

**Data Categories:**
- Demographic information
- Case management records
- Service delivery history
- Contact information
- Program enrollment data

**Processing Activities:**
- Data storage and retrieval
- Report generation
- Dashboard visualization
- Audit logging
- System maintenance

**Duration:** For the term of the Customer's subscription, plus retention period as specified in the Terms of Service

---

## Customer Instructions

Melken TechWork will process personal data only in accordance with:
1. This DPA
2. The CareAccess Terms of Service
3. Documented instructions from the Customer
4. Applicable data protection laws

Melken TechWork will inform the Customer if, in its opinion, an instruction violates applicable data protection laws.

## Data Protection Safeguards

### Technical Measures

**Encryption**
- ✅ TLS encryption for data in transit
- ✅ AES-256 encryption for data at rest
- ✅ Secure key management

**Access Controls**
- ✅ Role-based access control (RBAC)
- ✅ Unique user authentication
- ✅ Session management and timeout
- ✅ Multi-factor authentication (available)

**Monitoring**
- ✅ Comprehensive audit logging
- ✅ Real-time infrastructure monitoring
- ✅ Automated security alerts

---

### Organizational Measures

**Policies & Procedures**
- ✅ Documented security policies
- ✅ Incident response procedures
- ✅ Change management processes
- ✅ Employee training programs

**Access Management**
- ✅ Least-privilege access for staff
- ✅ Background checks for employees
- ✅ Confidentiality agreements
- ✅ Regular access reviews

**Tenant Isolation**
- ✅ Strict organizational data separation
- ✅ Database-level isolation
- ✅ Application-level access controls

## Data Subject Rights

Melken TechWork will assist the Customer in fulfilling data subject rights requests, including:

- **Access:** Providing access to personal data
- **Rectification:** Correcting inaccurate data
- **Erasure:** Deleting data upon request
- **Restriction:** Limiting processing
- **Portability:** Exporting data in a usable format
- **Objection:** Stopping certain processing activities

Melken TechWork will respond to Customer requests within a reasonable timeframe, typically within 14 days.

---

## Data Breach Notification

### Incident Response

In the event of a personal data breach, Melken TechWork will:

1. **Notify the Customer** without undue delay, typically within 72 hours of discovery
2. **Provide details** including:
   - Nature of the breach
   - Data categories and subjects affected
   - Likely consequences
   - Measures taken or proposed
3. **Assist the Customer** in fulfilling breach notification obligations
4. **Conduct investigation** and implement remediation measures

### Customer Responsibilities

The Customer is responsible for:
- Determining whether to notify data subjects or regulators
- Fulfilling legal notification requirements
- Coordinating with Melken TechWork on incident response

---

## Data Retention & Deletion

### Retention

Personal data is retained for the duration of the Customer's subscription, plus any retention period required by law or specified in the Terms of Service.

### Deletion

Upon termination of the subscription, Melken TechWork will:

1. **Export data** to the Customer (if requested)
2. **Delete or anonymize** all personal data within 30 days
3. **Provide confirmation** of deletion to the Customer
4. **Retain only** what is required by law or for legitimate business purposes (e.g., audit logs)

### Backup Retention

Backup copies may be retained for up to 90 days for disaster recovery purposes, after which they are securely deleted.

- Other legally recognized transfer mechanisms
