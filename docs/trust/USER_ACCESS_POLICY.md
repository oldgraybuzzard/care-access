# User Access & Data Use Policy

**CareAccess Platform**  
Owned & Operated by Melken TechWork

---

## Purpose

This policy defines who may access CareAccess, what they may access, and how they may use the information. It applies to all users of subscribing nonprofit organizations and Melken TechWork staff.

---

## Authorized Users

Access to CareAccess is limited to:

- ✅ **Employees** of subscribing organizations
- ✅ **Authorized contractors or volunteers** with proper credentials
- ✅ **Organizational leadership and administrators**

### Requirements

Each user must:
- Use **individual credentials** (no shared accounts)
- Complete **required privacy training**
- **Acknowledge this policy** before accessing the system

---

## Organizational Scope

Users may access **only data belonging to their organization**. Cross-organization access is blocked by design.

### Tenant Isolation

CareAccess is a multi-tenant SaaS platform with strict organizational data separation:
- Database-level isolation
- Application-level access controls
- No shared data between organizations
- Independent audit logs per tenant

---

## Role-Based Access Levels

### Field Staff / Caseworkers

**Access:**
- View assigned cases and summaries
- Access client contact information
- View service history for assigned cases

**Restrictions:**
- No bulk exports
- No administrative permissions
- No access to unassigned cases
- No cross-program visibility

---

### Supervisors / Program Leadership

**Access:**
- View program-level data
- Run reports and dashboards
- Access aggregated metrics
- Limited export permissions

**Restrictions:**
- No administrative permissions
- No access to other programs (unless authorized)
- Exports require justification and logging

---

### Organization Administrators

**Access:**
- Manage users and roles
- Review audit logs
- Approve exports
- Configure organizational settings

**Responsibilities:**
- Ensure permissions align with job responsibilities
- Review access regularly
- Investigate unusual activity
- Maintain user list accuracy

---

### Platform Administrators (Melken TechWork)

**Access:**
- Infrastructure and security management
- System monitoring and maintenance
- Performance optimization

**Restrictions:**
- **No routine access to customer data**
- Support access requires customer authorization
- All access is logged and reviewed
- Limited to technical operations

---

## Appropriate Use

### Permitted Activities

Access is permitted **only** for legitimate work purposes:
- ✅ Providing direct services to clients
- ✅ Program oversight and supervision
- ✅ Compliance and reporting
- ✅ Quality improvement
- ✅ Authorized research (with proper approvals)

### Prohibited Activities

The following activities are **strictly prohibited**:
- ❌ Browsing without a work-related purpose
- ❌ Accessing information out of curiosity
- ❌ Sharing credentials with others
- ❌ Exporting data without approval
- ❌ Using data for personal purposes
- ❌ Sharing data outside authorized channels
- ❌ Accessing data for non-work purposes

---

## Reporting & Exports

### Export Controls

Data exports are:
- **Logged** with user, timestamp, and scope
- **Limited** by role and permissions
- **Monitored** for unusual patterns
- **Subject to approval** for sensitive data

### Export Responsibilities

Users who export data must:
- Have a legitimate work purpose
- Store exports securely
- Delete exports when no longer needed
- Follow organizational data handling policies
- Never share exports via unsecured channels

---

## Monitoring & Enforcement

### Activity Monitoring

All user activity is monitored, including:
- Login/logout events
- Data access
- Report generation
- Export activities
- Administrative changes

### Audit Logging

Comprehensive audit logs capture:
- **Who** accessed the system
- **What** data was accessed
- **When** the access occurred
- **Where** the access originated (IP address)
- **Why** (context from user role and activity)

### Enforcement

Violations of this policy may result in:
- Access suspension
- Access termination
- Investigation
- Notification to organizational leadership
- Legal action (for serious violations)

Serious violations may trigger investigation and customer notification.

---

## User Acknowledgement

By accessing CareAccess, you acknowledge that:

1. This system contains **confidential information** about children and families
2. Access is **role-based, monitored, and logged**
3. Access is permitted **only for legitimate work-related purposes**
4. You will **protect the privacy and dignity** of all individuals in the system
5. You will **comply with all organizational policies** regarding data handling
6. Violations may result in **access termination and other consequences**

---

## Customer Responsibilities

Subscribing organizations are responsible for:

- **User Management:** Adding, removing, and updating user access
- **Role Assignment:** Ensuring permissions align with job responsibilities
- **Training:** Providing privacy and security training to users
- **Monitoring:** Reviewing audit logs for unusual activity
- **Incident Response:** Investigating and responding to policy violations
- **Policy Enforcement:** Taking appropriate action for violations

---

## Melken TechWork Responsibilities

Melken TechWork is responsible for:

- **Platform Security:** Maintaining secure infrastructure and access controls
- **Audit Logging:** Providing comprehensive activity logs
- **Incident Response:** Investigating security events and notifying customers
- **Support:** Providing authorized support with customer permission
- **Compliance:** Maintaining alignment with industry standards

This policy may be updated periodically to reflect:
